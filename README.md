# DirectoryAgent — AI Agents Vault

**Get your SaaS or AI tool listed on 100+ directories.** → **[aiagentsvault.com](https://aiagentsvault.com)**

DirectoryAgent is a done-for-you directory-submission service. You fill in your product details once; we handle getting it listed across 100+ vetted directories — building backlinks, growing domain rating, and driving organic traffic.

## What it does

- 🚀 **One-time setup** — submit product name, URL, tagline, description, and logo once.
- 🗂️ **100+ vetted directories** — we submit your product, spread over several weeks (mix of manual outreach and tool-assisted filling).
- 📊 **Live dashboard** — track each submission's progress in real time.
- 🔗 **Backlink add-on** — editorial placements on high-DR niche sites.

## Who it's for

SaaS and AI-tool founders who want backlinks and directory presence without doing hundreds of manual submissions.

## Tech stack

Astro 5 · Tailwind CSS · Supabase · Node.js + Playwright worker · Gemini 2.5 Flash · PayPal · Resend
